Seller Terms & Conditions

By registering as a seller on inwear, you agree to the following terms.

1. Product Quality & Listing

Sellers must ensure all uploaded products match their descriptions and images. Selling fake, damaged, or illegal items will result in immediate permanent account suspension.

2. Platform Fees & Payouts

  • Platform Fee: inwear charges a flat 10% commission on the final selling price of every successful order.
  • Wallet & Withdrawal: Your earnings will be credited to your virtual wallet. You can request a payout to your saved UPI ID.
  • Settlement Time: Funds become available for withdrawal after the 7-day return/replacement period is over.

3. Return & Replacement Policy

If a customer requests a valid return/replacement within 7 days (for defective or wrong products), the seller is responsible for bearing the return cost. The amount will be deducted from the seller's pending wallet.

4. KYC Verification

All sellers must submit valid identification documents for manual verification before their wallet is activated for payouts.
