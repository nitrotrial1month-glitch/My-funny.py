Welcome to inwear. Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Financial Data: UPI IDs for processing payouts (for sellers and delivery partners).
  • KYC Documents: Government-issued IDs (PAN/Aadhaar) strictly for verification to prevent fraud on the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data to process orders, facilitate deliveries, process payouts to your wallet, and verify the identity of our sellers and delivery partners. We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your KYC documents and financial data. Access is strictly limited to authorized admins only.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data, please contact our support team from the app dashboard.
