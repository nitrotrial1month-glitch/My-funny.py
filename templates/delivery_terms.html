Delivery Partner Terms

By registering as a Delivery Partner on inwear, you agree to abide by these rules.

1. Order Acceptance & Behavior

Once you accept an order, you are responsible for picking it up from the seller and delivering it safely to the customer. You must maintain professional behavior and wear neat attire while interacting with customers and sellers.

2. Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules

  • Cash Collection: For COD orders, you must collect the exact amount from the customer.
  • Cash Handover: You are strictly required to hand over the collected cash to the respective Seller.
  • Delivery Charge: Once the order is delivered and cash is settled, your delivery fee will be added to your inwear Wallet.

3. Payouts

Your accumulated delivery charges can be withdrawn from your wallet directly to your UPI ID via the dashboard. KYC verification is mandatory to enable withdrawals.

4. Account Suspension

Failure to deliver orders on time, misplacing cash, or tampering with products will result in immediate suspension and potential legal action.
